Prototype rulings

REALMS v0.7 engine

Health: 20. Deck: 30 unique faction cards. Hand: recurring selective cycle to 5 at the start of every round.

Resources: after the hand cycle, total the +1/+2 resource values on the five cards in hand. That is the round pool. Played cards do not reduce the already-generated total except by paying their cost. Unspent resource expires.

Placement: players alternate one play/pass at a time. Two consecutive passes move to Combat. Creatures may act the round they enter in this alpha.

Combat: players alternate one creature activation. Creature combat deals simultaneous damage. Direct attacks are legal unless Taunt or a Realm/card redirects them.

Initiative: determines the first Combat activation and alternates each round in this prototype. Blood Moon can override the opening cadence.

Deckout: each failed draw deals 1 Health in this prototype.

Deadlands half-cost: rounded up, minimum 1. Grave Echoes retain printed ability text, are 1/1, are accessible from the shared Grave State by either player, and never retrigger Deadlands when leaving play.

Open rule: Grave Echo deaths currently do count for faction/card death triggers, but not Realm triggers. The referee panel lets you override this manually if you want to A/B the opposite ruling.
