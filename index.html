REALMS v0.9 engine

Health: 20. Deck: 30 unique faction cards. Hand: recurring selective cycle to 5 at the start of every round.

Resources: after the hand cycle, total the +1/+2 resource values on the five cards in hand. That is the round pool. Played cards do not reduce the already-generated total except by paying their cost. Unspent resource expires.

Placement: players alternate one play/pass at a time. Two consecutive passes move to Combat. Creatures may act the round they enter in this alpha.

Combat: players alternate one creature activation. Creature-vs-creature damage is simultaneous: attacker STR and retaliation STR are snapshotted before either damage packet resolves.

Living Geodes: Pressure/Fold is retired. Prism cards cost 3, show exactly two colors, and attach to friendly Geodes. Choose one color when attached. Complete the Geode's printed color recipe to Crack it permanently; fused Prisms remain attached and active.

Harvest: Lifeblood/Nourished remains. Growth cards cost 3 and attach dormant to an ally. When their prerequisite happens they Bloom into multi-turn effects. Regurgitate and Consume remain the faction's two Major identities.

Continuum: Sequence remains a persistent 0–5 clock. FLUX cards cost 0 and sit armed in a visible Flux Zone. If their exact Sequence pattern happens during one round, they activate immediately. Up to 2 FLUX may be armed at once.

Moondemons: Bloodied and the universal 2-HP restore remain. Sigils cost 3 and attach face-down to a friendly Moondemon. Opponents know a Sigil is set, but it reveals only when its prerequisite is fulfilled.

Eliteborn: Equips remain their signature type. Creatures and attached Equips count toward Formation size. WST keeps 7 creature slots and Disperse; Rise now begins growing a Formation once it reaches size 2.

Initiative: determines the first Combat activation and alternates each round. Blood Moon can override the opening cadence.

Deckout: each failed draw deals 1 Health in this prototype.

Crystal Isle: at the end of every even-numbered round, Polarity simultaneously swaps every creature's current STR and current HP. Permanent, temporary, Equip, Prism, and other ongoing modifier layers remain attached and continue to function after the swap. A creature whose new HP is 0 is destroyed by Polarity. Each player tracks their own Polarity-caused deaths; reaching 5 loses the match. If both players reach 5 during the same Polarity Shift, the match is a draw.

Upper Strata: Overcharge resolves at end of round: each player loses 1 Health for every 2 unspent resources, rounded up. Chain Link is active while you control at least 3 creatures; when an enemy creature would deal combat damage directly to you, half that damage rounded down is assigned as evenly as possible across your creatures and the rest hits you. If Chain Link breaks while redirected packets are still unresolved, those packets return to you instead of disappearing.

Deadlands: when one of your non-Echo creatures dies, you may raise one creature from the shared Grave State as a 1/1 Grave Echo; either player may choose a creature originally owned by either player. Grave Echoes retain the original printed ability text and do not return to Grave State or retrigger Deadlands Realm effects when they leave play. Absorption: once during your Placement each round, if you control a Grave Echo, you may play a Creature from hand over it for that Creature's normal printed cost; the new Creature permanently absorbs the Echo's current STR and HP, not its printed effect text.

Alpha coverage: core faction systems and common card effects are automated. The referee controls remain available for edge-case text while the full 150-card implementation is hardened.
