REALMS v0.8 engine

Health: 20. Deck: 30 unique faction cards. Hand: recurring selective cycle to 5 at the start of every round.

Resources: after the hand cycle, total the +1/+2 resource values on the five cards in hand. That is the round pool. Played cards do not reduce the already-generated total except by paying their cost. Unspent resource expires.

Placement: players alternate one play/pass at a time. Two consecutive passes move to Combat. Creatures may act the round they enter in this alpha.

Combat: players alternate one creature activation. Creature-vs-creature damage is simultaneous: attacker STR and retaliation STR are snapshotted before either damage packet resolves.

Living Geodes: damage gives Pressure while Dormant or Cracked. Dormant reaching its Crack threshold transforms; Cracked reaching its Fold threshold dies. Provisional test rule: a Fracture chain cannot revisit the same Geode during that chain.

Harvest: creatures must gain STR or maximum HP from a friendly effect each round to remain Nourished; otherwise they Wither for 1 at round end. Regurgitate redistributes death; Consume concentrates printed Cost into STR or HP.

Continuum: Sequence is a persistent 0–5 clock advanced by new rounds and card effects, never by combat. Skip Ahead accelerates toward completion; Loop Back reverses after 5 for the 4 → 5 → 4 High Sequence window.

Moondemons: below max HP means Bloodied. Dealing damage to another target restores 2 HP. Frenzied grows while Bloodied but dies on returning to full HP; Bloodthirst bleeds Bloodied creatures and rewards natural bleed-out deaths.

Eliteborn: Creatures and attached Equips count toward Formation size. Each Creature can hold only one Equip. WST gets 7 creature slots and Disperse at Formation 5+; Rise grows Formations at size 3+ but one Creature death wipes that Formation.

Initiative: determines the first Combat activation and alternates each round. Blood Moon can override the opening cadence.

Deckout: each failed draw deals 1 Health in this prototype.

Deadlands: half-cost is rounded up, minimum 1. Grave Echoes are 1/1, retain printed ability text, are accessible from the shared Grave State by either player, and never retrigger Deadlands Realm effects when leaving play.

Alpha coverage: core faction systems and common card effects are automated. The referee controls remain available for edge-case text while the full 150-card implementation is hardened.
