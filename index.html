REALMS v0.8 engine

Health: 20. Deck: 30 unique faction cards. Hand: selective cycle to 5 at the start of every round.

Resources: your final five cards generate their printed +1/+2 resource values for the round. Unspent resource expires. FLUX cards cost 0; other cards keep their printed costs.

Placement: players alternate one play or pass. Two consecutive passes move to the Action Phase.

Action: players alternate one creature activation. Creature-vs-creature damage and retaliation are simultaneous. There is no separate defend/block action.

Living Geodes — PRISM: Pressure and Fold are removed. Attach Prisms to friendly Geodes, choose one of each Prism's two colors, and satisfy the creature's printed Crack Recipe. Prisms remain fused and active after Crack.

Continuum — FLUX: FLUX cards cost 0 and stay armed. If their printed three-number Sequence pattern occurs during one round, the FLUX activates and is discarded.

Moondemons — SIGIL: Sigils cost 3 and attach face-down to a friendly Moondemon. They reveal when their prerequisite occurs, resolve, then discard. Bloodied and the universal 2-HP restore remain active.

Harvest — GROWTH: Growths cost 3, attach face-up to a friendly Harvest creature, and Bloom when their prerequisite is fulfilled. Bloomed Growths can create ongoing effects. Lifeblood/Nourished/Wither remain active.

Eliteborn — EQUIP: Equips remain attached to creatures, count toward Formation size, and each creature may hold one Equip.

Initiative: determines the first Action activation and alternates each round. Realm effects can override normal cadence.

Deadlands: Grave Echoes remain 1/1 and use the shared Grave State. Deckout: each failed draw deals 1 Health.

Alpha status: v0.9 is a playable signature-system test build, not balance-locked. Card values and faction balance are expected to move after playtesting.
